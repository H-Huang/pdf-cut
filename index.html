Which format should I pick?
  • Kobo: pick Kobo KEPUB (or plain EPUB) and copy it to the reader over USB. Kobo can also open the cropped PDF.
  • Kindle: send the EPUB with Send to Kindle, or copy the cropped PDF over USB.
  • Other readers and apps: EPUB works almost everywhere. CBZ suits comic readers and KOReader.
  • EPUB, KEPUB and CBZ store each page as an image sized for your device. The cropped PDF keeps the original vector text, so you can still select and search it.
PDF Cut runs offline in your browser. Built with pdf.js, pdf-lib and JSZip.
